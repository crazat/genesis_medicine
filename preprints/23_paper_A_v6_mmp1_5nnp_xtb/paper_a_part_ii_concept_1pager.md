## Title (가제, draft)

**"Indapamide as a Pleiotropic MMP-1 Modulator across Korean Cohorts: Retrospective Mining of Dementia, Cardiovascular Mortality, and Skin/Melanoma Outcomes via HIRA-NPS, NHIS-Senior, and KFACS Linkage"**

## One-Paragraph Concept

Following the paper_A v6 computational pipeline (Zenodo D14 deposit 2026-05-30, *J Med Chem* full submission D60) which atomistically rationalized **vorinostat** and **indapamide** as MMP-1 active-site repositioning candidates with zero quantitative ChEMBL332 records, paper_A Part II tests the **clinical translational signal** of indapamide's hypothesized MMP-1 pleiotropy through three Korean cohort retrospective linkages.

**Tier-1 primary scope** (3 organ systems, mechanism precedent strongest, statistical power confirmed):
1. **Cognitive/Dementia** — NHIS-Senior n=558K + KFACS n=3,014 biobank linkage; outcome ICD-10 F00 + F01 + F03 + G30, exposure ATC C03BA11; expected HR detection 0.85 with 1-β=0.80 at α=0.05 in 5-year follow-up; HYVET-COG meta HR 0.87 (p=0.045) sets the prior; mechanism: MMP-1 mediates BBB ECM degradation, an early step preceding amyloid/tau (Yue CNS Neurosci Ther 2024).
2. **Cardiovascular mortality ≥80yr** — NHIS-Senior + KAMIR 87K registry; outcome ICD-10 I21 + I50 + I64, 1:4 propensity-matched control (HCTZ + ARB); HYVET 2008 ≥80yr Korean external validation (target *European Heart Journal* or *Hypertension*).
3. **Skin/melanoma** — HIRA-NPS 2009-2024 + KCCR linkage; outcome ICD-10 C43 (melanoma) + C44 (NMSC) + L57 (UV damage); active-comparator design vs HCTZ (Park 2020 PMC7373969 Korean precedent); Korean Indapamide-specific signal currently 0 papers — first-mover.

**Tier-2 supplementary**: Cancer composite (KCPS-II + KCCR), Dental Periodontal (KNHANES VII-VIII + HIRA K05.3, hypothesis-generating scope only).

## Why This Matters

- **paper_A v6 computational layer** establishes the *atomistic* binding-mode rationale for indapamide-MMP-1 direct engagement (Boltz-2 cofold + 3-NNP cross-val Pearson r=0.9146 95% CI [0.817, 0.973]; ΔE_relax 6.42-7.48 kcal/mol within predictable-conformer regime, NOT a σ-outlier).
- **paper_A Part II clinical layer** would be the first Korean cohort retrospective testing whether 1-2% population-level indapamide exposure (estimated from HIRA prescription patterns 2009-2024) produces measurable pleiotropy signals on 3 organ systems with mechanism-strongest precedents.
- **The Periostat® FDA 1998 28-year safety precedent** (sub-antimicrobial doxycycline as direct MMP-1 host-modulation therapy) provides the regulatory class-precedent justification for chronic mild MMP-1 modulation by indapamide.
